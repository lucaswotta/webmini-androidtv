## Built for Android TV

- Navigate with your remote. Move the pointer with the D-pad, click with OK and scroll at the screen edges.
- Browse with fewer interruptions. Block ads and trackers, confirm pop-ups and keep browsing in one tab.
- Keep your sites close. Save favorites with their titles, then hold OK to reorder or remove them.
- Find your way through a page. Search its text, jump a screen at a time or go straight to the top or bottom.
- Control playback. Use play, pause, rewind and fast-forward on the remote.
- Make it fit. Choose your search engine, text size, desktop site view and screen margins.

## Start with these controls

| Remote | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad + OK | Move the pointer and click |
| Up at the top of a page | Reach the address bar and toolbar |
| Back | Leave a field, close a menu or return to the previous page |
| Hold OK on a favorite | Open its actions |
| Page / channel keys | Jump through a long page |
| Media keys | Play, pause or seek 10 seconds |
